GlowEdge: The 4th Bottom Side for Ambilight TVs

Most smart TVs only feature ambient glow on 3 sides, leaving a dark void underneath. GlowEdge wirelessly closes the gap for full 360° 4-sided immersion — zero cameras on your bezel, zero HDMI sync boxes, and no visible wall wires.

Why GlowEdge is Different

Engineered from day one as clean, non-invasive technology for home theater enthusiasts.

🚫📷

Zero Ugly Screen Cameras

Cheap RGB kits stick an ugly camera pole atop your TV screen that captures room glare and ruins your interior. GlowEdge queries raw color frames directly from the TV's internal picture engine over Wi-Fi.

🎮⚡

Zero HDMI Sync Boxes (4K 120Hz VRR)

Expensive HDMI boxes ($300+) bottleneck modern gaming features (VRR, ALLM, 4K 120Hz, Dolby Vision). GlowEdge never touches the video cable — seamlessly working with internal TV apps (Netflix, HBO, YouTube) and PS5 / Xbox Series X.

🔌🔋

Direct TV USB Power (5V 0.9A)

Featuring our proprietary Dynamic Power Limiter, GlowEdge stays strictly within the TV's 900 mA USB limit. It boots and turns off in sync with your TV — no extra power bricks needed.

🧠✨

2D Depth Gradient Engine

The bottom glow is never a dull single-color average! The engine blends corner hues while injecting 20% projection from the top screen columns, generating true depth.

⚙️⏱️

Hardware RMT DMA (60 FPS)

Powered by ESP32-S3 / C3. Employs hardware RMT pulse generation with zero CPU stutter, delivering instantaneous color reaction and cinematic gamma correction.

🌐📲

Built-in Web Portal & Wireless OTA

Easy browser setup at http://ambilight.local. Effortless over-the-air firmware updates and real-time LED diagnostics.

Hardware Engineering & Power Flexibility

Industrial-grade fail-safe LED strip design, intelligent USB port detection, and dual-mode power engineering.

⚡🔌

Dual Power Modes: TV USB vs Turbo Charger

Eco 900mA / 4.5W Turbo 15W / 3A

Choose between a zero-wire stealth setup powered directly by your TV, or turbocharge brightness with an external USB adapter.

  • TV USB Mode (Eco 900mA / 4.5W): Direct power from TV USB 3.0. Automatic on/off sync with TV power state. Dynamic Current Limiter ensures 100% safety for the TV motherboard.
  • External Charger Mode (Turbo 15W / 3A): Unlocks 100% unrestricted peak brightness on pure white scenes, high-density LED strips (144/m), and heavy silicone neon diffusers.
🛡️✨

WS2813 Backup Data Line & ~7W/m Efficiency

Dual-Data Line (DI+BI) ~7W / Meter Typical

Industrial reliability with dual data redundancy and efficient power draw under real viewing conditions.

  • Dual-Data Redundancy (DI + BI): Unlike fragile WS2812B strips where one failed chip kills the entire chain, WS2813 features an integrated backup line. If any single LED breaks, the signal bypasses it without interruption.
  • Real-World Efficiency: Dynamic movie and gaming scenes consume only ~7W per meter, keeping power footprint minimal and temperatures cool.
🌈💡

Modular LED Densities & Silicone Neon Flex

30 / 60 / 144 LED/m Silicone Neon COB

Tailor your installation with customizable diode density and continuous diffusion optics.

  • Density Options: 30 LED/m (Eco TV USB), 60 LED/m (Cinema Golden Standard), or 144 LED/m (Liquid Studio Blending).
  • Silicone Neon Flex / COB Diffuser: Converts discrete LED hot-spots into an ultra-smooth, continuous neon light bar. (Recommended with USB Charger mode; throttles to safe 900mA on TV USB).
🔍🧠

Intelligent USB Port Detection Architecture

USB BC 1.2 DCP/SDP Web Portal Control

How GlowEdge automatically detects your power source to protect hardware and optimize brightness.

  • USB BC 1.2 Hardware Sensing: Distinguishes between Standard TV Ports (SDP, 500-900mA) and Dedicated Charging Ports (DCP, shorted D+/D- <200Ω or divider resistance).
  • Web Portal Power Budget Control: Select Auto-Detect, TV USB (900mA Max), or Wall Charger (3A Max) in the browser portal (http://ambilight.local) to match your custom setup.

Transparent Facts: What GlowEdge Does & Does NOT

No marketing fluff. Clear, honest engineering expectations.

🟢 What GlowEdge Solves

  • Fills the missing 4th bottom edge for TVs featuring 3-sided factory ambient lighting (OLED 7/8 series, The One, The Xtra).
  • Zero lag & flawless sync: Queries the TV's internal API at up to 20-30 FPS and smoothly interpolates to 60 FPS on LED hardware.
  • Clean aesthetics: Hidden discreetly behind the bottom TV bezel without hanging wires.
  • Power Safety: Built-in smart power budget prevents TV USB port overload during full white scenes.

🔴 What GlowEdge Does NOT Do

  • Does NOT create lighting on standard TVs from scratch: Requires a Philips TV with built-in Ambilight and open picture processing API.
  • Does NOT replace a 50W brick on 85″ screens: Under 0.9A USB power, peak full-screen white is smoothly scaled. An optional 15W USB-C charger can be added for unlimited 100% white blasting.
  • Requires JointSpace API support: Compatible with Google TV, Android TV, and supported Titan OS models.
