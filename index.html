Why GlowEdge is Different

Engineered from day one as clean, non-invasive technology for home theater enthusiasts.

🚫📷

Zero Ugly Screen Cameras

Cheap RGB kits stick an ugly camera pole atop your TV screen that captures room glare and ruins your interior. GlowEdge queries raw color frames directly from the TV's internal picture engine over Wi-Fi.

🎮⚡

Zero HDMI Sync Boxes (4K 120Hz VRR)

Expensive HDMI boxes ($300+) bottleneck modern gaming features (VRR, ALLM, 4K 120Hz, Dolby Vision). GlowEdge never touches the video cable — seamlessly working with internal TV apps (Netflix, HBO, YouTube) and PS5 / Xbox Series X.

🔌🔋

Direct TV USB Power (5V 0.9A)

Featuring our proprietary Dynamic Power Limiter, GlowEdge stays strictly within the TV's 900 mA USB limit. It boots and turns off in sync with your TV — no extra power bricks needed.

🧠✨

2D Depth Gradient Engine

The bottom glow is never a dull single-color average! The engine blends corner hues while injecting 20% projection from the top screen columns, generating true depth.

⚙️⏱️

Hardware RMT DMA (60 FPS)

Powered by ESP32-S3 / C3. Employs hardware RMT pulse generation with zero CPU stutter, delivering instantaneous color reaction and cinematic gamma correction.

🌐📲

Built-in Web Portal & Wireless OTA

Easy browser setup at http://ambilight.local. Effortless over-the-air firmware updates and real-time LED diagnostics.

Transparent Facts: What GlowEdge Does & Does NOT

No marketing fluff. Clear, honest engineering expectations.

🟢 What GlowEdge Solves

  • Fills the missing 4th bottom edge for TVs featuring 3-sided factory ambient lighting (OLED 7/8 series, The One, The Xtra).
  • Zero lag & flawless sync: Queries the TV's internal API at up to 20-30 FPS and smoothly interpolates to 60 FPS on LED hardware.
  • Clean aesthetics: Hidden discreetly behind the bottom TV bezel without hanging wires.
  • Power Safety: Built-in smart power budget prevents TV USB port overload during full white scenes.

🔴 What GlowEdge Does NOT Do

  • Does NOT create lighting on standard TVs from scratch: Requires a Philips TV with built-in Ambilight and open picture processing API.
  • Does NOT replace a 50W brick on 85" screens: Under 0.9A USB power, peak full-screen white is smoothly scaled. An optional 15W USB-C charger can be added for unlimited 100% white blasting.
  • Requires JointSpace API support: Compatible with Google TV, Android TV, and supported Titan OS models.
